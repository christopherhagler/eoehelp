## 1. About this policy

This is the Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy that Washington's My Health My
Data Act requires. It is separate from the general
[privacy policy](/privacy) on purpose: Washington requires a distinct document
about health data, and folding it into a general policy would not satisfy the
law. Nevada's SB370 asks for much the same, and this policy covers both.

**Consumer health data** means information that identifies your past, present,
or future physical or mental health, including anything we could reasonably
link to you and anything inferred from what you enter. In eoehelp, that is nearly everything you
enter: it is a record of a disease and how it is being treated.

eoehelp is run by one person, Christopher Hagler, in Alabama, United States, at
[POSTAL ADDRESS]. Questions and requests:
[privacy@eoehelp.org](mailto:privacy@eoehelp.org).

**These rights are offered to everyone who uses eoehelp, wherever they live.**
Washington and Nevada require them; deciding what someone is entitled to from
where they say they live would be error-prone and worse for the patient, so the
same rights are given to all.

## 2. The consumer health data we collect, and why

Table: Consumer health data in eoehelp

| Category | Examples | Why it is collected |
|---|---|---|
| Symptoms | Whether you ate solid food, whether food went down slowly or got stuck, what you did about it, pain on swallowing, emergency visits for stuck food | This is the daily record, and the basis of the symptom score |
| Food and ingredients | Meals you log, the ingredients in them, packaged products you scan, and the allergen groups those imply | To keep the diet record that elimination and reintroduction depend on |
| Medications | Which medications you take, doses, schedules, when you took or skipped one, and why a course ended | To show treatment alongside symptoms, and to work out how often doses were taken |
| Procedures and results | Endoscopy dates and findings, biopsy eosinophil counts, dilations, and the facility if you enter it | To keep the clinical history that only your records hold |
| Diagnosis and profile | The month of diagnosis, your year of birth, sex at birth if you give it | Diagnosis context, the age check, and research summaries if you ever opt in |
| Free-text notes | Anything you write in a note field | Because a record without your own words is thinner than one with them |
| Consent and activity records | Which document you agreed to and when, and which of your records were read or changed | Evidence that consent was given, and a trail of access to your health data |

It is collected for one purpose: to keep this record for you, to show it back to
you, and to produce summaries you can take to your clinician. It is not used for
advertising, for profiling, or for any purpose you did not ask for.

## 3. Where it comes from

All of it comes from you.

- You type it into the app.
- When you scan a barcode, your device's camera reads it in your browser. No
  image and no video leaves your device; only the decoded number is sent, so
  that a product's label can be looked up.

There is no other source. Nothing is purchased, imported from a data broker,
received from a health care provider, or inferred from anywhere outside your own
entries.

## 4. What we share

Your consumer health data is not shared, with these exceptions, both of which
you control or which are necessary to run the service:

- **Service providers processing on our behalf**, once any are in use. They
  are listed in the next section, and their contracts will require that they
  act only on instructions and never use your data for their own purposes.
  eoehelp is not yet running on deployed infrastructure, so none is in use
  today.

There is no way to share your record from inside eoehelp. When one is added,
this policy will describe it before it works.

Your consumer health data is not shared with any affiliate, because there is no
corporate group: eoehelp is one person. It is not shared with advertisers,
insurers, employers, or data brokers.

The food lookups deserve a precise description, because they are the only case
where something related to what you ate leaves the service. When you search for
a product or scan a barcode, our server, not your browser, asks Open Food Facts
and USDA for the product's label. The request carries the search text or the
barcode and nothing else: no account, no identifier, no symptom, nothing that
connects it to you or to anyone. They cannot tell who asked, or that the same
person asked twice. It is disclosed here because it relates to food you are
about to record, and because disclosure is the safer answer when the question is
arguable.

## 6. Your rights, and how to use them

You have the right to:

- **Confirm** whether we collect, share, or sell your consumer health data.
- **Get a copy** of it, including a list of every third party it has been
  shared with.
- **Withdraw consent** to its collection and to its sharing.
- **Delete** it. When you ask, it is deleted from the live service, and any
  processor would be instructed to delete it too. Deletion runs on a shorter
  deadline than the response times below, and it reaches backups; the backup
  retention period will be stated here once eoehelp runs on deployed
  infrastructure, and set so that the deadline is met.

To exercise any of these, email
[privacy@eoehelp.org](mailto:privacy@eoehelp.org) from the address on your
account, or write to [POSTAL ADDRESS]. Because the email address on the account
is how you sign in, a request from it is how a request is authenticated; if
there is doubt, you may be asked to confirm from that address before anything is
released or erased.

You will get a response within 45 days. If more time is genuinely needed, that
can be extended once by a further 45 days, and you will be told why within the
first 45.

If a request is refused, the refusal will say why and how to appeal it. An
appeal is read by the operator and answered within 45 days. If the appeal is
refused as well, you may complain to the Washington State Attorney General
through their online complaint form.

## 7. Consent, and no sale of your data

- Consent to collection is asked for separately and specifically, before
  collection, and is never bundled with anything else. The three documents at
  sign-up are three separate checkboxes for that reason.
- Nothing is shared today except with the processors named above, so no
  separate sharing consent is collected. If sharing is added, it will be asked
  for separately.
- Consent is never a condition of getting something unrelated, and no feature
  is withheld for declining an optional one.
- **We do not sell consumer health data.** There is no exchange of it for money
  or anything of value, and none is planned. A sale would require a signed
  authorization from you, separate from this policy and containing everything
  the statute prescribes. None is sought and none exists.
- Sharing your record for research does not exist yet. If it is built, it will
  be a separate, specific, opt-in consent, with a choice of categories and the
  ability to withdraw.

## 8. No geofencing

eoehelp does not collect or use location data of any kind, and operates no
geofence around any health care facility, pharmacy, or any other place.

## 9. Nevada residents

Under Nevada SB370 you may ask what consumer health data is collected, ask for
it not to be sold, and ask for it to be deleted. eoehelp does not sell consumer
health data. Use the same address as above, and the same response times apply.
