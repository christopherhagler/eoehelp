## 2. What eoehelp is

eoehelp is a personal health record for eosinophilic esophagitis. You enter what
happened; it stores it, shows it back to you, and produces summaries you can take
to your own clinician.

It is not a medical record system, it is not a medical device, and it is not a
substitute for care. It is not connected to any clinic or hospital, and no
clinician has an account. Today there is no way to share your record from
inside eoehelp: you read it on screen, and you show it to your clinician
yourself. If a way to share a summary is added, these terms will say so before
it works.

## 3. This is not medical advice

Nobody reviews what you enter. No clinician watches this service, and no alert
goes anywhere.

Nothing in eoehelp tells you what to do about your treatment or your diet. The
summaries describe the entries you made. Decisions about your care belong to you
and your gastroenterologist, and this record should support that conversation
rather than replace it.

**If you cannot swallow, think food is stuck, or are in pain, contact your
doctor or emergency services. eoehelp is not monitored and nobody will see what
you enter.**

## 8. What the record can and cannot tell you

eoehelp shows and summarises what you typed. It cannot verify any of it. Days
you did not log, or logged late, change what the summaries say.

The food-pattern analysis counts days in your own diary. Because eosinophilic
inflammation can be active while you feel well, **no result in this app can show
that a food is harmless to you, or that your disease is inactive**. The strongest
thing it says is that no pattern was seen in the days you recorded.

A symptom score measures symptoms. It does not measure inflammation, and a
falling score is not proof of healing. Only your clinician, with the tests they
order, can tell you that.
