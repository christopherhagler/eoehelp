For people living with eosinophilic esophagitis

Walk into your next appointment with the whole story.

EoE is managed on months of evidence — how often food sticks, which eliminations held, what the last biopsy showed. Most of that lives in your head, and a fifteen-minute appointment is not long enough to rebuild it. eoehelp keeps the record, and turns it into a summary your gastroenterologist can actually use.

Help research, only if you decide to

EoE research is short of exactly the data patients generate every day — especially which foods fail on reintroduction, and why. If you opt in, a de-identified version of your record can contribute to that.

It is a separate, specific decision. Never a default, never bundled into the terms of service, and reversible at any point.

What contributing means

  • check Your name, email, and free-text notes are never included.
  • check Dates are shifted, so intervals between events stay accurate while the actual dates do not identify you.
  • check Researchers see grouped cohorts, never one person's record, and sign an agreement not to attempt re-identification.
  • check You choose which categories to share, and can withdraw later.
